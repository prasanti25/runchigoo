# Legal pages: publication checklist

Privacy and Terms are styled, routed and connected to real support functionality. They are deliberately marked as preview drafts, not approved legal policies. No final effective date or invented business identity has been published.

Before public launch, the business owner and qualified counsel should confirm:

- Registered operating entity, business address, support ownership and designated privacy/grievance contact.
- Applicable territory, customer eligibility, merchant/courier contracts, allocation of responsibilities, dispute process and mandatory consumer rights.
- Serviceable areas, fee/tax rules, GST invoicing requirements, cancellation/refund eligibility, processing workflows and timelines. Implement the approved behavior before promising it.
- A data inventory and purpose-specific retention schedule for accounts, addresses, historical orders, payments, location, support, reviews, logs and backups.
- Access, correction and deletion procedures; identity verification that does not solicit unnecessary documents; required response/escalation processes. The current privacy ticket is only a request intake mechanism.
- Hosting, email, Gemini, Razorpay and Maps provider settings, processing locations, applicable provider contracts and cross-border requirements. Review the Gemini project's actual retention/data-use terms before enabling it.
- Applicable Indian privacy/consumer/e-commerce obligations and any other jurisdictions the product will serve. These pages do not certify DPDP or other legal compliance.
- Final document versions, publication/effective dates, change notifications and server-side policy-version acceptance records. The current registration checkbox is a frontend check, not an auditable consent ledger.
- Security and authorization review, provider configuration, incident handling and named operating owners.

Current implementation safeguards include limited partner contact fields, optional GPS sharing, click-to-load Google maps, AI input disclosure, no app storage of full card credentials, customer-owned privacy tickets and audited review moderation. These are functional safeguards, not proof of full compliance or production security.

Relevant pages: `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/contact`, `/faq`, `/support?category=privacy`, `/settings`.

Do not remove the draft notice just to make a launch appear complete. Update the pages to match the approved policies and implemented behavior, then obtain the appropriate review.
